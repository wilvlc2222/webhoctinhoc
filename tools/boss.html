Dev tool. For each grade and topic it loads the topic's lessons in hidden frames the way thuthach.html does, and counts the questions left after that page's filters (NEEDS_PAGE and OPTIONS_BELOW are read from thuthach.html itself). The extra questions of assets/js/bossbank.js are counted too. A topic needs 20 questions (20 points). Result also in window.bossShort and window.bossData. Add ?list=1 to print the questions the filter dropped, ?g=4 to check one grade only.
